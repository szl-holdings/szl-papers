## Justification per code

### Primary candidates

**`03B30` (Foundations / reverse mathematics)** — The thesis is, at its core, a *foundations*
exercise: it asks **exactly which axioms** (A1–A5) are necessary and sufficient to force the Λ
aggregator, and it does so via a machine-checked Lean 4 kernel. The reverse-mathematics framing —
"what is the weakest axiom set that proves Λ-uniqueness" — and the honest finding that A1–A4 are
*insufficient* (requiring A5) is precisely a foundations-of-a-theory result. **Best primary if the
paper is routed to math.LO.**

**`68T27` (Logic in artificial intelligence)** — Strong alternative primary. The substrate is an
AI-governance system whose guarantees are expressed as logical proof obligations discharged in a
theorem prover. **Best primary if the paper is routed to cs.AI / cs.LG.** (`68T01` "general AI"
and `68T07` "machine learning" from the task brief are broader and less precise than `68T27`; we
prefer `68T27` because the contribution is logic-of-AI, not a new learning algorithm.)

### Why the chosen secondaries

**`39B22` (Functional equations for real functions)** — The Λ-uniqueness chain reduces to an
n-dimensional **Cauchy functional equation** (`multiplicative_monotone_isPow`, the symmetric and
topology branches). This is the literal mathematical engine of the paper (Kolmogorov 1930, Nagumo
1930, Aczél 1948, HLP 1934). **This is the most mathematically specific code in the paper** and is
arguably co-primary; we place it first among secondaries.

**`91B03` (Mechanism design theory)** — The VCG truthfulness results (`vcgDominantStrategyTruth`,
`vcgIndividualRationality`) are textbook mechanism design, formalized in Lean.

**`94A60` (Cryptography)** — DSSE-signed receipts, BLS aggregation, Rekor Merkle inclusion, Fulcio
chains, SLSA provenance, and the Round-10 crypto formalizations sit squarely in cryptography.

**`68T27` (Logic in AI)** — Carries the AI-governance framing when `03B30` is primary.
