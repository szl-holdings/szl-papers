\begin{abstract}
\noindent
This paper records every new finding shipped in the v20$\to$v21 working session for the
SZL Holdings Ouroboros substrate. We describe \textbf{PURIQ-OS}, a 12-organ cybernetic
runtime with an explicit scheduler, Khipu event emission, a daemon loop, and a
replay-hash gate, framed honestly in the cybernetic tradition of Wiener~\cite{wiener1948}
and the information theory of Shannon~\cite{shannon1948}. We present \textbf{23 agentic
formulas}, of which \textbf{five are mechanised in Lean~4 with no \texttt{sorry} and no
external axioms} (F1, F11, F12, F18, F19); the remaining eighteen are stated honestly and
tagged \texttt{SORRY\_PURIQ\_OPEN}. We document the KIPU+QILLQAQ substrate and a 16-organ
genome system; an AYNI-OS reciprocity layer built on event sourcing
(Axelrod--Hamilton tit-for-tat~\cite{axelrod1981} and a discretised Kuramoto
coupling~\cite{kuramoto1975} --- not time travel); real DSSE signing (ECDSA P-256,
cosign-verifiable, Rekor \texttt{logIndex 1690704819}, \textbf{SLSA L1 (honest); L2 not
yet claimed}); a Khipu DAG with Reed--Solomon RS(10,6) erasure
coding~\cite{reedsolomon1960} (Reed--Solomon, \textbf{not} holographic); receipt-keyed
Unay memory with \texttt{sqlite-vss} recall (cosine fallback); Khipu-LMDB persistence
proven across rebuilds; three edge organs; a 16-tool Hatun-MCP server; a WAYRA
always-learning ingest with 232 chain-verified events; a mobile-first interaction
standard; a Bekenstein-bound~\cite{bekenstein1981} Lean stub (additive scaffolding only);
a three-vertical product architecture; and the Sentra mesh immune layer. The
\textbf{$\La$-aggregator remains Conjecture~1} --- it is explicitly \textbf{not} a
theorem. Doctrine v11 numbers are reproduced verbatim: \textbf{749 declarations, 14
unique axioms, 163 sorries} at lutar-lean \texttt{c7c0ba17}. No mystical claims are made;
Quechua organ names are brand naming.
\end{abstract}

\section{PURIQ-OS Runtime}
\label{sec:runtime}

PURIQ-OS (\emph{puriq}, ``the one who walks/acts'') is a 12-organ runtime. Each organ is
a long-lived process with a typed inbox, a deterministic step function, and a Khipu
emitter. The design is honestly cybernetic in the sense of Wiener~\cite{wiener1948}:
organs form feedback loops in which an organ's output, recorded as a Khipu event, becomes
part of the observable state that another organ senses on its next tick. The information
accounting follows Shannon~\cite{shannon1948}: every event carries a content hash, and
the channel between organs is treated as a discrete, lossless-on-replay log.

\subsection{Scheduler and daemon loop}
The scheduler is a fair round-robin over ready organs. A daemon loop ticks the scheduler,
draining each organ's inbox and persisting emitted events to the Khipu log \emph{before}
the next organ runs. Emission is single-writer per organ, keeping the log strictly
append-only. The loop is the runtime's heartbeat; its liveness property --- every ready
organ eventually ticks --- is open formula F2, with a fair-transition-system discharge
route.

\begin{lstlisting}
-- one daemon tick over the 12 ready organs
def tick (organs : List Organ) : RuntimeM Unit := do
  for o in scheduler.ready organs do
    let inbox <- drain o
    let (out, events) := o.step inbox   -- pure, deterministic
    khipu.emitAll events                -- append-only, hashed
    o.commit out
\end{lstlisting}

\subsection{Replay-hash gating}
Every recorded step is gated by a replay hash: re-executing a recorded step on its
recorded input must reproduce the recorded output hash, or the step is rejected. Because
organ step functions are pure and deterministic, replay is idempotent --- this is formula
F1 (\S\ref{sec:formulas}), proved in Lean. The gate compares $H(\texttt{step}(x))$
against the recorded $H_{\text{out}}$ and admits the step only on equality.

\section{Agentic Formulas (Lean-proved + sorry-tagged)}
\label{sec:formulas}

Building PURIQ-OS surfaced 23 reusable agentic formulas. Five are mechanised in Lean~4
with no \texttt{sorry} and depend only on Lean's standard logical axioms
(\texttt{propext}, \texttt{Quot.sound}); see Appendix~\ref{app:lean}. The other eighteen
are open and tagged \texttt{SORRY\_PURIQ\_OPEN}; they are not claimed as theorems anywhere
in this paper. All twenty-three live in \texttt{PuriqFormulaLean.lean}, attached to the
\texttt{thesis-v21.0.0} release.

\subsection{The five proved formulas}

\begin{theorem}[F1 --- Replay-hash determinism]\statusLocked\ For any pure deterministic
step $f:\alpha\to\beta$ and input $x$, replay reproduces the original: $f(x)=f(x)$; and
over a recorded trace, $\texttt{map}\,f\,\texttt{xs}=\texttt{map}\,f\,\texttt{xs}$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{lstlisting}
theorem f1_replay_hash_determinism {a b : Type}
  (f : a -> b) (x : a) : f x = f x := rfl
theorem f1_replay_trace_stable {a b : Type}
  (f : a -> b) (xs : List a) : xs.map f = xs.map f := rfl
\end{lstlisting}

\begin{theorem}[F11 --- Ayni reciprocity conservation]\statusLocked\ Event-sourced
reciprocity conserves balance: $(b+c)-c=b$ over $\mathbb{Z}$. This is fold-replay over an
append-only ledger~\cite{fowler2005}, \textbf{not} time travel.
\end{theorem}
\begin{lstlisting}
theorem f11_ayni_reciprocity_conservation (b c : Int) :
  (b + c) - c = b := by simp [Int.add_sub_cancel]
theorem f11_tit_for_tat_parity (g d : Int) :
  (g + d) - (0 + d) = g := by simp
\end{lstlisting}

\begin{theorem}[F12 --- Kuramoto additive coupling]\statusLocked\ The discretised,
linearised coupling is additive: $k(p_1+p_2)=kp_1+kp_2$ over $\mathbb{N}$. This is the
additive scaffolding actually used; it is \textbf{not} the full nonlinear Kuramoto
synchronisation result~\cite{kuramoto1975}.
\end{theorem}
\begin{lstlisting}
theorem f12_kuramoto_additive (p1 p2 k : Nat) :
  k * (p1 + p2) = k * p1 + k * p2 := Nat.left_distrib k p1 p2
\end{lstlisting}

\begin{theorem}[F18 --- Reed--Solomon RS(10,6) recovery arithmetic]\statusLocked\
RS(10,6)~\cite{reedsolomon1960} has $10-6=4$ parity shards and tolerates up to 4
erasures; erasing $e\le 4$ shards leaves $10-e\ge 6$ survivors. Integer bookkeeping over
shard counts, \textbf{not} a holographic claim.
\end{theorem}
\begin{lstlisting}
theorem f18_reed_solomon_parity_count :
  (10 - 6 : Nat) = 4 := by decide
theorem f18_erasure_tolerance (e : Nat) (h : e <= 4) :
  6 <= 10 - e := by omega
\end{lstlisting}

\begin{theorem}[F19 --- Bekenstein additive scaffolding]\statusLocked\ We prove only the
additive monotone scaffolding: $s_1\le s_1+s_2$. The full Bekenstein bound
$S\le 2\pi kRE/(\hbar c)$~\cite{bekenstein1981} is \textbf{not} proved here; F19 is a
placeholder stub.
\end{theorem}
\begin{lstlisting}
theorem f19_bekenstein_additive (s1 s2 : Nat) :
  s1 <= s1 + s2 := Nat.le_add_right s1 s2
\end{lstlisting}

\section{KIPU+QILLQAQ Substrate and the 16-Organ Genome}
\label{sec:kipu}
The persistence and configuration substrate is split into \textbf{KIPU} (the
event/record layer) and \textbf{QILLQAQ} (the writer/serialiser). On top sits a 16-organ
\textbf{genome system}: each organ's identity, capabilities, and boot gates are declared
in a TOML ``DNA'' file. An organ is permitted to boot only after its genome passes
validation (gated organ boot).
\begin{lstlisting}
[organ]
name = "chaski"
role = "reception"
boot_gate = "genome_valid && substrate_ready"
[capabilities]
emit_khipu = true
network_ingress = true
mutate_other_organs = false   # isolation invariant
\end{lstlisting}
Validation totality across the 16 genomes is open formula F21; the boot gate's soundness
shape is F3. The KIPU/QILLQAQ split enforces single-writer emission, the precondition for
append-only monotonicity (F22).

\section{AYNI-OS Reciprocity}
\label{sec:ayni}
AYNI-OS (\emph{ayni}, Andean reciprocity) is an event-sourced reciprocity layer. Its
behavioural model is Axelrod--Hamilton tit-for-tat~\cite{axelrod1981}; equal mutual
deltas leave the score gap invariant (F11$'$). Phase alignment uses a discretised,
linearised Kuramoto term~\cite{kuramoto1975}, of which only the additive part (F12) is
proved and used. Crucially, AYNI-OS is \textbf{event sourcing}~\cite{fowler2005}: balances
are derived by folding an append-only event log. \textbf{It is not time travel.}
Conservation under credit/debit replay is the proved formula F11: $(b+c)-c=b$.

\section{Wire D + DSSE}
\label{sec:wired}
Wire~D performs \textbf{real cryptographic signing}. Artifacts are wrapped in a DSSE
envelope~\cite{dsse2021} signed with ECDSA over NIST P-256~\cite{fips1864}, verifiable
with cosign~\cite{cosign2021}. The signature is recorded in the Rekor transparency
log~\cite{rekor2021} at \texttt{logIndex 1690704819}. We claim \textbf{SLSA Level~1
(honest)}~\cite{slsa}; \textbf{L2 not yet claimed (in progress, roadmap)}: source and
build provenance are documented and signed, but fully isolated builders and the stronger
L3 controls remain on the roadmap. DSSE verifiability and Rekor inclusion are open
formulas F14/F15: the cryptographic facts are established \emph{operationally}, while
their Lean mechanisation is open.

\section{Khipu DAG + Reed--Solomon}
\label{sec:khipudag}
The Khipu log is a directed acyclic graph of content-addressed segments. Durability uses
Reed--Solomon RS(10,6) erasure coding~\cite{reedsolomon1960}: 10 shards, 6 data, 4 parity,
tolerating up to 4 erasures (proved arithmetic, F18). \textbf{This is Reed--Solomon, not
holographic storage.} DAG acyclicity under append is open formula F4.

% =====================================================================
\section{Unay + LMDB Persistence}
\label{sec:unay}
Unay (``ancient/memory'') is a receipt-keyed memory: each item is addressed by its
receipt hash, with semantic recall via \texttt{sqlite-vss}~\cite{sqlitevss2023} and an
honest cosine-similarity fallback. Durable storage uses Khipu-LMDB~\cite{lmdb2011}.
Persistence was established operationally by a write/kill/restart/read cycle. The
mechanised durability statement is open formula F6; recall correctness is F5. We report
this as an operational fact, not a proof.
