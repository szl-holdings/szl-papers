May--June 2026 innovation rounds into the canonical thesis line. Its central

\section{Related Work: Means Characterizations, Mechanism Design, and Provenance}

v22 sits at the intersection of three precisely-cited literatures.

\subsection{Quasi-arithmetic means and the A1--A4 insufficiency}
A review of published results on quasi-arithmetic and symmetric means confirms that
A1--A4 are \emph{insufficient} to force the geometric mean. Kolmogorov (1930) and
Nagumo (1930) gave the quasi-arithmetic-mean characterizations; Acz\'el (1948) gave
the functional-equation route to means; Hardy, Littlewood \& P\'olya (1934)
developed the power-mean family; and Voorneveld (2008) characterized aggregators
that admit asymmetric weights. None of these force a \emph{symmetric} geometric mean
from A1--A4 alone, which is exactly why an extra invariance hypothesis (A5) is
required.

\subsection{Mechanism design and truthful auctions}
The VCG truthfulness results follow the mechanism-design tradition (Vickrey 1961): a
mechanism is dominant-strategy incentive compatible if truthful reporting is optimal
regardless of others' reports, and individually rational if participation never
yields negative utility. The geometric-mean trust aggregator itself is independently
motivated as a consensus weighting in the pairwise-comparison literature (Cs\'at\'o
2018). The evolution-of-cooperation literature (Axelrod \& Hamilton 1981) motivates
why truthful participation must be made the rational strategy.

\subsection{Supply-chain provenance and verified kernels}
Build-provenance posture is graded against SLSA v1.0; v22 verifies attested
build-service provenance with \texttt{slsa-verifier}, reaching \textbf{L2}. The Lean
mechanisation discipline follows the verified-systems tradition of the Lean~4 prover
(de Moura \& Ullrich 2021), the information-flow security model of Goguen \&
Meseguer (1982), and the hash-chain integrity tradition of Merkle (1987).

\section{A5 Axiom Merge --- the A1--A4 Uniqueness Gap, Corrected (MERGED, PR \#148)}

The historical ``Theorem~1 --- $\La$ is the unique aggregator under A1--A4'' was
\textbf{incorrect}. We exhibit the monster, name the guilty lemma, and revise the
theorem.

\subsection{The verified counterexample}
The asymmetric mean $\Phi(x_1,x_2)=x_1^{2/3}\,x_2^{1/3}$ satisfies homogeneity (A2),
boundedness (A4), continuity (A1), and idempotence (A3), yet $\Phi\neq\La_2$ and
$\Phi(2,1)=2^{2/3}\neq 2^{1/3}=\Phi(1,2)$, so permutation invariance fails. A
second, discrete machine-checked counterexample is the max-aggregator: at the point
$(4,1)$ the max returns $\max=4$ while $\La_2(4,1)=\sqrt{4}=2$, so they disagree.
Both refutations are reproduced verbatim from the locked defense and are
machine-checked.

\begin{remark}[Refutation, machine-checked]
Under $\{$A1--A4$\}$ alone, $\La$ is \textbf{not} the unique aggregator:
$\Phi(x_1,x_2)=x_1^{2/3}x_2^{1/3}$ is a distinct A1--A4 mean. The missing hypothesis
is symmetry (permutation invariance), which we add as A5. Even under the full
$\{$A1--A5$\}$, unconditional uniqueness remains \textbf{FALSE} without the further
declared block-consistency axiom A6$'$ (v23); $\La$ is therefore \textbf{Conjecture
1}.
\end{remark}

\subsection{The fix: A5 as a structure field, not a new axiom}
The fix landed on main on 2026-06-03 via PR \#148: we add an
\texttt{IsPermutationInvariant} predicate and an \textbf{A5 structure field} to the
\texttt{LutarAxioms} record. The lemma \texttt{Lambda\_A5\_perm\_invariant} is
\textbf{sorry-free}, proved with \texttt{Equiv.prod\_comp} /
\texttt{Fintype.prod\_equiv}. Because A5 is a structure field on an existing record
rather than a fresh \texttt{axiom} declaration, the \textbf{unique-axiom count stays
14}; the live corpus moves to \textbf{794 declarations / 14 unique axioms / 191
sorries} (measured \texttt{974e5e0c}, 2026-06-03 17:32Z). This distinction ---
\emph{adding structure vs.\ adding an axiom} --- is the load-bearing honesty point of
the section: the trusted base did not grow.

\begin{lstlisting}[language=]
-- A5 added as a STRUCTURE FIELD (not a new axiom)
structure LutarAxioms (k : Nat) where
  continuous    : ...          -- A1
  homogeneous   : ...          -- A2
  idempotent    : ...          -- A3
  bounded       : ...          -- A4
  permInvariant : IsPermutationInvariant Phi   -- A5 (new field)

theorem Lambda_A5_perm_invariant : IsPermutationInvariant Lambda := by
  intro sigma; simpa using Fintype.prod_equiv sigma _ _ (by simp)
-- #print axioms Lambda_A5_perm_invariant  => [propext, Quot.sound]
\end{lstlisting}

\subsection{Why symmetry is the right missing hypothesis (measurement-theoretic frame)}
The counterexample does not merely show that \emph{some} axiom is missing; it shows
that the missing hypothesis is \textbf{symmetry}, and symmetry is independently
motivated before any uniqueness theorem is invoked. Trust sub-scores are naturally
\textbf{ratio-scale} quantities: they have a natural zero (total absence of
conformance) and a natural unit (the conformance baseline), so ``twice as
trustworthy'' is meaningful. Measurement theory establishes that the appropriate
central tendency for ratio-scale data is the \textbf{geometric mean}, because it is
covariant under the admissible ratio-scale transformation $x\mapsto r\cdot x$:
$\La(r\cdot x)=r\cdot\La(x)$, whereas the arithmetic mean is covariant only up to an
additive shift that is not a ratio-scale transformation.

Permutation invariance (A5) is the formal statement that the \emph{labels} on the
trust sub-scores carry no information --- only their multiset of values does. In a
governance reading: the trust verdict must not depend on the order in which an
auditor lists the evidence axes. The asymmetric counterexample
$\Phi(x_1,x_2)=x_1^{2/3}x_2^{1/3}$ privileges the first axis over the second ---
exactly the manipulability A5 forbids. This is why A5 is a \emph{structure field} the
system always intended, surfaced explicitly by the refutation, rather than a new
idealization bolted on to rescue a theorem.

\section{The Axiom-Count Invariant Under the A5 Merge}

Because the unique-axiom count is a load-bearing honesty number, we treat its
invariance under the A5 merge as a claim that itself deserves an explicit argument
rather than an assertion. The question a hostile reviewer asks is blunt: \emph{you
added a hypothesis to your characterization; how can the axiom count not go up?}

\subsection{Structure field vs.\ axiom declaration}
In Lean~4, an \textbf{axiom} is a declaration introduced with the \texttt{axiom}
keyword that the kernel accepts without proof; it enlarges the trusted base and
appears in \texttt{\#print axioms} for every theorem that depends on it. A
\textbf{structure field}, by contrast, is a component of a record type: it is a
\emph{hypothesis a caller must supply a proof for}, not a fact the kernel grants for
free. Adding A5 as the field \texttt{permInvariant : IsPermutationInvariant Phi} on
\texttt{LutarAxioms} means any theorem quantifying over \texttt{LutarAxioms} now
carries an additional \emph{premise}, discharged by the caller, exactly as a
mathematician adds a hypothesis to a lemma. No new fact is granted axiomatically; the
trusted base is unchanged.

\subsection{What \texttt{\#print axioms} reports}
The lemma \texttt{Lambda\_A5\_perm\_invariant} --- the proof that $\La$ itself
satisfies A5 --- reports \texttt{[propext, Quot.sound]} under \texttt{\#print
axioms}, i.e.\ only Lean-core logical axioms, no project-specific idealization.
Consequently the corpus-wide unique-axiom count stays at \textbf{14}: A5 contributes
a provable property of $\La$, not an article of faith. The declaration count rises
(749 $\to$ 794 live) because the field, its predicate, and the supporting lemmas are
new declarations; the \emph{axiom} count does not, because none of them is an
\texttt{axiom}.

\begin{remark}[The honesty point in one line]
Adding structure is free of trust cost; adding an axiom is not. A5 adds structure, so
the trusted base is unchanged at 14 unique axioms --- and we say so in every
\texttt{\#print axioms} output rather than asking the reader to take it on faith.
\end{remark}

\section{VCG Mechanism Truthfulness (Proven on Branch, PR \#172)}

Both VCG sorries are closed on branch (PR \#172, pending merge). We report them as
\textbf{in-review, branch-proven} --- sorry-free on the branch but not yet part of
the locked kernel.

\begin{theorem}[\texttt{vcgDominantStrategyTruth} --- in review] \statusBranch
In the trust-weighted VCG mechanism, truthful reporting of an agent's private
valuation is a \textbf{dominant strategy}: for every agent and every profile of
others' reports, the agent's utility under truthful reporting is at least its utility
under any misreport. Proved on branch with \texttt{Finset.exists\_max\_image} and
\texttt{Finset.add\_sum\_erase}; sorry-free on PR \#172, not yet merged to main.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[\texttt{vcgIndividualRationality} --- in review] \statusBranch
Participation in the mechanism never yields negative utility: each agent's
equilibrium utility is non-negative, so a rational agent always prefers to
participate. Proved on branch with the same Mathlib lemmas; sorry-free on PR \#172,
not yet merged to main.
\end{theorem}

\subsection{The trust-weighted allocation setting}
The mechanism in which Theorems 6.1--6.2 hold is a trust-weighted
Vickrey--Clarke--Groves allocation. Each agent reports a private valuation for a
governed action (for example, the right to emit a high-privilege Khipu event); the
mechanism allocates to the agent maximizing reported social value and charges each
winner the externality it imposes on the others --- the classical VCG payment rule.
The ``trust'' weighting enters by scaling each agent's reported value by its
$\La$-aggregated trust score, so that an agent with a low conformance history is
discounted before the allocation is computed. The dominant-strategy property is what
makes the mechanism safe to deploy in an adversarial multi-agent setting: no agent
can improve its outcome by misreporting, so the governance layer does not need to
model strategic lying. The individual-rationality property guarantees that honest
participation is never punished, which is the precondition for voluntary compliance.

Two honesty caveats apply. First, the proofs are \textbf{branch-only}: until PR \#172
merges, these are in-review results, not locked-kernel theorems. Second, the
truthfulness guarantee is about the \emph{mechanism}, not about the trust scores
themselves --- a strategyproof allocation over manipulated trust inputs is still only
as trustworthy as the $\La$-aggregation feeding it, which is exactly why the $\La$
uniqueness question (Conjecture~1) and the mechanism truthfulness question are
reported as two separate, independently-labeled strands of the convergence.

conditional uniqueness requires \emph{one} further sufficient axiom. We surveyed the
published characterizations and report the candidate routes honestly, each with its
independent governance motivation and its relative strength. v23 declares A6$'$
(block-consistency) as the chosen route; v22 records the menu.

\end{center}

The common algebraic skeleton is: any route that pins \emph{quasi-arithmetic}
structure, combined with homogeneity (A2, already present), automatically selects the
geometric mean, because the only homogeneous quasi-arithmetic means are the power
means and positive homogeneity picks the exponent-1 power mean --- the geometric
mean. The routes differ only in how wide a gap they leave between premise and
conclusion; A6$'$ is preferred precisely because it is phrased entirely in terms of
the audit \emph{process} and never mentions means or exponents, so the geometric mean
falls out as a theorem rather than being assumed.

\begin{remark}[Why this is still Conjecture 1]
Each route above is \emph{sufficient} for conditional uniqueness, but each requires
its declared axiom. v22 has merged A5 and partially closed the Cauchy\_ND chain; it
has \textbf{not} discharged any of these further axioms on main. Until one is both
declared and its chain closed with a green Lake CI, $\La$ uniqueness is
conditional-at-best and the unconditional claim stays machine-checked FALSE. Hence
Conjecture~1.
\end{remark}

\section{Methodology: the Lakatosian Discipline of Correction}

v22 is, methodologically, a case study in correcting a refuted conjecture in the
open.

\subsection{Monster-barring, done honestly}
The historical ``Theorem~1'' was a \emph{naive conjecture} in Lakatos's sense; the
asymmetric mean $\Phi$ is the \emph{monster} that refutes it. The dishonest response
would be monster-barring by redefinition --- quietly narrowing ``aggregator'' until
$\Phi$ is excluded without saying so. Instead we \textbf{exhibit} the monster,
\textbf{name} the guilty lemma (the unstated symmetry assumption), and state the
\textbf{revised} conjecture (uniqueness under $\{$A1--A5$\}$ + A6$'$) with its
remaining gap disclosed. The machine-checked counterexample is the mechanism that
\emph{causes} the Conjecture~1 label: were unconditional uniqueness true, the system
would not so label $\La$.

\subsection{Why a disclosed obligation beats a hidden assumption}
By a process-reliabilist reading of formal epistemology, a ``proved'' label is
trustworthy only if the labeling process has a high accuracy rate. A corpus that
calls everything ``proved'' carries less information in its ``proved'' label than one
that also accurately says ``conjecture'' and ``in review''. The single residual
Cauchy\_ND sorry, the branch-only status of the VCG lemmas, and the in-review status
of the Round~10--11 modules are therefore reported as such. This is the same
discipline the drift gate enforces mechanically: the locked counts 749 / 14 / 163 at
\texttt{c7c0ba17} are numbers the gate refuses to let move silently, so that no
experimental obligation can be mistaken for a kernel-verified fact.

\begin{remark}[The convergence in one sentence]
v22 converges three previously-deferred strands --- the corrected $\La$
axiomatization (A5 added, uniqueness still open), branch-proven mechanism
truthfulness, and attested SLSA L2 provenance --- without upgrading a single
conjecture to a theorem or moving a single locked count.
\end{remark}
