\begin{abstract}
Autonomous and agentic artificial intelligence is \emph{post-deterministic}:
the same prompt, model, and tools can produce different action sequences, and
the controlling logic is statistical rather than fixed. Deployed into regulated
and defense settings, such systems break the classical assumption that auditing
means re-deriving a deterministic trace. The governance question is no longer
``what is the unique correct output?'' but ``under what authority did this
system act, on what aggregated evidence of trust, and can the record be tampered
with after the fact?'' This thesis unifies the SZL Holdings research program
(v1--v25, DOI-pinned) into a single framework we name \textbf{Governed
Post-Determinism (GPD)}: a discipline for bounding, attesting, and replicating
the behavior of post-deterministic agents so that every governed action carries
a checkable, tamper-evident warrant.

GPD rests on \textbf{five pillars}, each mapped to an exact, honestly-tiered
result. (P1)~Protocol-Bounded Execution, anchored by F1 replay determinism
(locked). (P2)~Verifiable Intent-to-Execution, anchored by a \emph{locked
triad}---F4 (the Khipu receipt DAG is acyclic and stays acyclic under append),
F7 (the Chaski channel delivers FIFO, positionally), and F22 (the emit log is
append-only and monotone)---plus F18 (Reed--Solomon coding) and attested
supply-chain provenance (DSSE/cosign, Sigstore, SLSA Build L1+L2 where it runs).
(P3)~Bounded-Recursion Control Plane: the Lutar invariant $\Lam$ (equal-weight
geometric mean); its unconditional uniqueness is \textbf{Conjecture~1 ---
machine-checked FALSE}, while its conditional uniqueness under
slice-multiplicativity is \textbf{Theorem~U --- PROVEN, axiom-free}.
(P4)~Semantic Quorum Assurance: Khipu BFT; unconditional safety is
\textbf{Conjecture~2 --- OPEN}, conditional agreement under $n\ge 3f+1$ and
honest non-equivocation is proven axiom-clean (\texttt{khipu\_quorum\_safety\_conditional}).
(P5)~Epistemic State Replication.

The unifying observation is a \textbf{single structural pattern}: in each
pillar, the honest result is a \emph{conditional theorem whose antecedent is the
weakest checkable hypothesis}, and the unconditional statement sits at a
\emph{machine-checked sharp boundary}. We hold the SZL honesty doctrine binding:
locked-proven $=$ \textbf{exactly eight} formulas
$\{$F1,\,F4,\,F7,\,F11,\,F12,\,F18,\,F19,\,F22$\}$ (the count is itself a
theorem, \texttt{locked\_count\_eight}, by \texttt{decide}); the locked-kernel
baseline at \texttt{c7c0ba17} (749/14/163) is unchanged; the ${\sim}185$
experimental CI-green theorems are a separate tier, never folded in.

\section{Introduction: the post-deterministic governance gap}

Classical software governance assumes determinism: an auditor can re-run a
program on recorded inputs and obtain the recorded outputs, so correctness is
\emph{re-derivable}. Agentic AI dissolves this assumption. A large-model agent
that plans, calls tools, and revises its own plan is \textbf{post-deterministic}:
its control flow is sampled, its environment is non-stationary, and the ``same''
task may yield different --- yet individually defensible --- action sequences.
Re-derivation no longer establishes accountability.

This is not a defect to engineer away; it is the nature of capable autonomy. The
governance question changes shape: it is no longer sufficient to answer ``who did
what?''; one must answer \emph{why} an action occurred and \emph{under what
authority}, with a record an auditor cannot dispute (ISACA, ``The Growing
Challenge of Auditing Agentic AI,'' 2025). The strongest current framing is an
\emph{enforceability ladder} whose top rung --- ``Two-Plane Verified'' ---
demands evidence captured \emph{outside the agent's own process boundary}, the
only rung yielding proof a regulator cannot dispute (ARMO, ``AI Agent
Governance''). The broader call for AI developers to make verifiable claims to
which they can be held accountable was articulated by Brundage et al.,
``Toward Trustworthy AI Development'' (arXiv:2004.07213).

\textbf{Governed Post-Determinism (GPD)} is SZL Holdings' answer: a theory and a
substrate for wrapping a post-deterministic agent in checkable bounds, attested
records, and replicated state, so that the \emph{non-determinism is permitted but
the governance is not}. The thesis of this paper is that the entire SZL corpus
--- the Ouroboros loop, the Lutar invariant $\Lam$, the Khipu consensus, and the
receipt/provenance layer --- is \textbf{one framework with five pillars}, with a
single mathematical signature developed in \S\ref{sec:pattern} and proven across
\S\S\ref{sec:p3}--\ref{sec:p1p5}.

\subsection{The five pillars of GPD}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Protocol-Bounded Execution (P1)} --- autonomy runs inside a
terminating, well-founded protocol; the loop, not the single inference, is the
unit of governance.
\item \textbf{Verifiable Intent-to-Execution (P2)} --- every governed decision is
a signed, append-only receipt, and the producing artifact carries attested
supply-chain provenance.
\item \textbf{Bounded-Recursion Control Plane (P3)} --- multi-axis trust evidence
is aggregated to a single bounded verdict by $\Lam$, with a machine-checked
uniqueness boundary.
\item \textbf{Semantic Quorum Assurance (P4)} --- distributed organs reach
agreement under Byzantine fault tolerance, with a machine-checked safety
boundary.
\item \textbf{Epistemic State Replication (P5)} --- replicated organs maintain a
consistent governed-state record built from P2 receipts and the P4 quorum.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{What this paper claims, and what it does not}
This paper claims a \textbf{unification}, not a new headline theorem. Every
mathematical fact it relies on already exists in the SZL corpus at a pinned
commit, tiered honestly. The novelty is (i)~the GPD framework as the organizing
theory, (ii)~the structural-pattern observation of \S\ref{sec:pattern} that the
pillars share, and (iii)~the locked-eight update incorporating the genuine
F4/F7/F22 proofs. We make no claim that GPD ``solves'' AI governance, that $\Lam$
is a theorem unconditionally, or that Khipu is unconditionally safe.

\section{The unifying structure of GPD: the checkable-antecedent pattern}\label{sec:pattern}

This section states the conceptual core of the unification. It is a
\emph{meta-observation about the corpus}, not a new Lean theorem; it is the lens
through which the proven results of \S\S\ref{sec:p3}--\ref{sec:p1p5} become
\emph{one} result.

\subsection{The pattern}
\begin{quote}
\textbf{The GPD checkable-antecedent pattern.} For each governance primitive of a
post-deterministic system, the \emph{universal} (unconditional) guarantee one
would like is either \textbf{machine-checked false} or \textbf{provably
impossible}. The honest, reachable guarantee is a \textbf{conditional theorem}
whose antecedent is the \textbf{weakest checkable property of the object} under
which the universal guarantee becomes true --- and the gap between
conditional-true and unconditional-false is a \textbf{machine-checked sharp
boundary}.
\end{quote}

The pattern instantiates twice, with mathematically independent proofs but
identical epistemic shape:

\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}p{1.6cm}p{3.0cm}p{2.3cm}p{3.0cm}p{2.7cm}@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Pillar} & \textbf{Universal claim} & \textbf{Status} & \textbf{Checkable antecedent} & \textbf{Conditional result} \\
\midrule
P3 / $\Lam$ & $\Lam$ is the unique A1--A5 aggregator & FALSE (\texttt{maxAgg\_ne\_Lambda}) & slice-multiplicativity & Theorem~U: \texttt{lambda\_unique\_of\_separable}, axiom-free \\
P4 / Khipu & two quorums never certify conflicting verdicts & impossible if $n\le 3f$ (LSP) & honest non-equivocation, $n\ge 3f+1$ & \texttt{khipu\_quorum\_safety\_conditional}, axiom-clean \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

The deep claim of GPD is that \textbf{these two checkable antecedents are the
same kind of object}. Slice-multiplicativity is a property of the aggregator's
functional form an auditor can confirm by inspecting $\Phi$. Honest
non-equivocation is a property of the organs' signing behavior an auditor can
confirm by checking one signed vote per organ per action. In both cases the
antecedent is verifiable \emph{from the artifact}; without it the universal claim
fails at a machine-checked boundary; with it the universal claim becomes a
theorem with $\texttt{\#print axioms} \subseteq \Ucoreaxioms$ --- no project
axiom.

\subsection{Why this is the right theory of governance}
Governing a post-deterministic system is \emph{not} proving it always behaves
correctly --- that is the false universal. It is \textbf{engineering the system so
the checkable antecedents hold, and then attesting they held}. P3 governs
\emph{what verdict} ($\Lam$ under slice-multiplicativity); P4 governs
\emph{whether replicas agree} (Khipu under honest non-equivocation); P1 governs
\emph{that the loop terminates}; P2 governs \emph{that the record is checkable};
P5 governs \emph{that the record is consistent across replicas}.

\subsection{The honesty corollary}
Because the universal claims are false/impossible, any artifact that
\emph{asserts} them asserts a machine-checked falsehood. The doctrine ---
``$\Lam$ is Conjecture~1,'' ``Khipu safety is Conjecture~2'' --- is the only
statement consistent with the kernel. Honesty here is a theorem of the system,
not a marketing posture.

\section{Pillar P3 --- the Bounded-Recursion Control Plane: the Lutar invariant}\label{sec:p3}

\subsection{Definition and the aggregation axioms}
\begin{definition}[$\Lam$]
For $x\in[0,1]^k$, $\Lam_k(x) = \left(\prod_{i=1}^{k} x_i\right)^{1/k}$ --- the
equal-weight geometric mean. Its defining governance behavior is
\emph{zero-absorption / weakest-link}: a single fully-failed axis ($x_i=0$)
vetoes trust ($\Lam=0$). Hence \textbf{trust is never $100\%$} unless every axis
is perfect; the verdict is conjunctive.
\end{definition}

Candidate aggregators $\Phi:[0,1]^k\to[0,1]$ are constrained by five axioms
(Lean: \texttt{LutarAxioms}): (A1)~monotonicity, (A2)~positive homogeneity,
(A3)~idempotence, (A4)~boundedness, (A5)~permutation-invariance.

\subsection{The unconditional refutation --- Conjecture 1 is machine-checked FALSE}
\begin{theorem}[Refutation of unconditional uniqueness; machine-checked FALSE; CI-green]
There exists $\Phi\ne\Lam_k$ satisfying A1--A5. The max-aggregator
$\mathrm{maxAgg}(x)=\max_i x_i$ satisfies A1--A5 yet differs from $\Lam$: at
$(4,1)$ on the ratio scale, $\mathrm{maxAgg}=4$ while $\Lam_2(4,1)=2$. The
minimum aggregator is a second witness.
\end{theorem}
\noindent Lean witness: \texttt{Lutar.Round13.maxAgg\_ne\_Lambda} (Lean-core
axioms only). Therefore \emph{unconditional} $\Lam$-uniqueness is
\textbf{Conjecture~1}, never a theorem.

\subsection{Theorem U --- the conditional uniqueness, PROVEN axiom-free}
\begin{theorem}[Theorem U; experimental, axiom-free; CI-green]\label{thm:U}
Let $\Phi$ satisfy \{A1, A2, A3, A5\} and be \emph{slice-multiplicative}: $\Phi$
factors as $\Phi(x)=\prod_i f_i(x_i)$ with each $f_i$ multiplicative, normalized,
and monotone. Then $\Phi=\Lam_k$.
\end{theorem}
\noindent Lean witness: \texttt{Lutar.Round13.lambda\_unique\_of\_separable}, with
$\texttt{\#print axioms} = \Ucoreaxioms$ --- \emph{no project axiom}. Theorem~U
removes the A6$'$ project axiom that v23 required; a governed-AI aggregator
uniqueness whose trusted base is the bare Lean core is, as far as we can
determine, new.

\subsection{The sharp boundary (CUT-1 representation)}
The CUT-1 representation work (Waves 18--22, experimental, axiom-free) closes the
characterization on its stated hypotheses, making precise where conditional-true
meets unconditional-false. Slice-multiplicativity is the weakest checkable
antecedent: dropping it readmits \texttt{maxAgg}.

\section{Pillar P4 --- Semantic Quorum Assurance: Khipu BFT consensus}\label{sec:p4}

\subsection{The model (honest: faulty organs may equivocate)}
Reasoning organs vote on a verdict; up to $f$ may be Byzantine and may
equivocate. Khipu is the quorum-intersection consensus over signed votes.

\subsection{The unconditional claim --- Conjecture 2, OPEN}
Unconditional Byzantine safety (no two quorums ever certify conflicting verdicts
under \emph{arbitrary} adversary behavior) is \textbf{Conjecture~2}, open. We
never claim it.

\subsection{The conditional theorem --- agreement / no split-brain, axiom-clean (Wave 23)}
\begin{theorem}[Conditional agreement; experimental, axiom-clean; Wave 23]\label{thm:khipu}
Under $n\ge 3f+1$ and \emph{honest non-equivocation} (each honest organ signs at
most one vote per action), any two certified quorums intersect in an honest organ
and therefore cannot certify conflicting verdicts; the committed verdict is
unique.
\end{theorem}
\noindent Lean witness: \texttt{Lutar.Wave23.khipu\_quorum\_safety\_conditional}
(\texttt{Lutar/Wave23/QuorumSafety.lean}, 5 declarations axiom-clean,
$\texttt{\#print axioms} = \Ucoreaxioms$). This reuses classical quorum
intersection (Lamport--Shostak--Pease; PBFT) but supplies the Lean
witness-extraction discharging the old \texttt{sorry}; it is conditional only.

\subsection{Prior art (positioning, not GPD source)}
Quorum intersection and BFT safety descend from Lamport--Shostak--Pease, PBFT
(Castro--Liskov), HotStuff, and the Coq-formalized Velisarios; GPD reuses the
intersection argument and adds the Lean conditional witness.
