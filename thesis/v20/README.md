## What this version is

v20 is the **culmination** of the expansion-then-consolidation arc. It takes the
per-theorem verified index delivered by v19 and presents the whole substrate as one
**anatomical body** — a finite set of named organs, each with a typed interface, a
deterministic step semantics, and an explicit ledger of which properties are
kernel-verified, which are operational facts, and which are open.

- **The twelve-organ anatomy** — Puriq, Khipu, Qillqaq, Unay, Ayni, Wayra, Chaski,
  Wallpa, Wasi-Rikuq, Hatun, Sentra, Hatun-Mind. Organs communicate only through the
  append-only, content-addressed Khipu log, so the body's observable state *is* the log.
- **The verified spine** — the v18 thesis-theorem track (`TH_V18_01`–`TH_V18_16`),
  read verbatim from the corpus index. Eleven of seventeen indexed modules are sorry-free
  over Lean-core axioms; one (`TH_V18_14`) is sorry-free but axiom-gated (two disclosed
  SHA-256 axioms); five carry honestly-recorded open obligations.
- **The five locked formulas mapped onto the organs** — F1 (replay determinism), F11
  (Ayni reciprocity), F12 (Kuramoto additive), F18 (Reed–Solomon RS(10,6)), F19
  (Bekenstein additive).
- **Three-class claim discipline** — every organ property is tagged *proved*,
  *operational fact*, or *open*.

## What this version is NOT

v20 introduces **no new proven mathematics** and closes no `sorry` that was not already
closed at `c7c0ba17`. Its contribution is the anatomical *presentation* and the
claim-class tagging that v19's verified index made possible. The conditional Λ-uniqueness
theorem, the machine-checked refutation of unconditional uniqueness, and the VCG proof
are all later (v22/v23) results and are **not** claimed here.

## Honest posture (load-bearing)

- The **Lutar invariant Λ is Conjecture 1 — unconditionally, NEVER a theorem.**
- **Locked / proven = 5:** `{F1, F11, F12, F18, F19}`. F12/F19 prove only **additive
  scaffolding** fragments, never the full physical theorems (Kuramoto, Bekenstein).
- **Disclosed idealizations** — the SHA-256 axioms in `TH_V18_14` are declared and
  printed in the `#print axioms` ledger.
- **SLSA L1 + L2 attested; NOT L3.**
- **Operational facts are not proofs** — DSSE/Rekor/LMDB/Wayra are reproducible
  operational facts, not machine-proved theorems.
- Quechua organ names are **brand naming**, not prior-art or cultural claims. No mystical
  or physical-reality claims are made.
